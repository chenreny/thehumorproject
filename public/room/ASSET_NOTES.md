# Campus After Hours room assets

`campus-room-v5.webp` is an illustrated decorative background generated with the built-in imagegen tool from the user-approved `design/mockups/campus-after-hours-desktop-v5.png`. The interface was removed from the artwork; all text, navigation, memes and controls are rendered as live HTML.

Generation prompt: Preserve v5's hand-painted navy wall, detailed plant at the left edge, tall wooden window with illuminated campus dome at the right edge, articulated desk lamp and warm wooden desk. Remove the navigation, heading, central meme frame, reactions, thumbnails and taped prompt note. Fill removed areas with uninterrupted textured navy wall. Keep the center empty for live page content. No text or interface elements.

`radio-v5.webp` is a separate transparent illustrated navigation asset made with the built-in imagegen tool. Prompt: Extract and faithfully recreate only v5's blue boombox radio with carrying handle, two speakers, cream crown decoration and coral expressive strokes. Preserve the painted ink-and-gouache style, isolate on transparency, and omit all text and other page elements.
