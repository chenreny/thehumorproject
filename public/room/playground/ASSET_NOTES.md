# Campus diorama comparison assets

Generated for the separate development-only `/playground` comparison page with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Existing room artwork and animal photographs are unchanged. Generated originals remain in Codex's generated-images directory; the project consumes the WebP copies here. `sharp` converted the world with quality 92 and the sprite atlas losslessly, preserving alpha.

- `campus-diorama-v1.webp`: 1672 × 941 painted cutaway campus world, without animals or UI baked into the illustration.
- `animal-sprites-v1.webp`: 1536 × 1024 transparent atlas, three columns and two rows of 512 × 512 cells. Top row: library cat, courtyard raccoon, subway dog. Bottom row: dorm cat, dining cat, walking visitor raccoon. The atlas has actual zero-alpha background pixels.
- `campus-diorama-mobile-v1.webp`: 1086 × 1448 (3:4) portrait recomposition of the desktop world for phones, converted at quality 92.
